How to read the result

What reproduced

  • The same four foundations derived straight from the statement: the height model, odd subset sums, fixed-count sums, and the two-cup lemma.
  • 5 of his 6 approaches, including the editorial one, with matching key nodes.
  • The same implementation hazards: 64-bit heights up to 4·10¹⁰, exact square roots, printing heights rather than indices.

What differed

  • His P5 did not come up in our run; two approaches of ours (P2, P5) did not come up in his.
  • We split some steps into two nodes (offsets, the canonical-family lemma) and built small cases from two cups instead of four or five.
  • Many prerequisite edges differ between matched nodes: the explorer wires routes slightly differently each run.
